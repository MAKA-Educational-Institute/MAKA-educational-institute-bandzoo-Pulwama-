Our School

Maulana Abul Kalam Azad Educational Institute was established in 2006. The school is located in the rural area of Bandzoo, Pulwama, Jammu and Kashmir.

The school is a co-educational institution offering education from Grades 1 to 7, with an attached pre-primary section.

English is the medium of instruction and the academic session begins in April.

The school is approachable by an all-weather road and operates from a rented school building.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality education in a supportive and disciplined environment while helping students develop knowledge, confidence, creativity and good values.


🌱 Our Vision

To encourage every student to learn, grow and become a responsible and capable member of society.
