Maulana Abul Kalam Azad Educational Institute

Maulana Abul Kalam Azad Educational Institute is an educational institution located at Bandzoo, Pulwama, Jammu and Kashmir.

The school focuses on providing students with quality education while encouraging discipline, knowledge, confidence, creativity and good values.

Our aim is to provide a supportive learning environment where students can develop academically and personally.

Our Mission

To provide meaningful education and help students develop the knowledge, skills and values required to become responsible and capable members of society.


Our Vision

To create a positive and inspiring educational environment where every student gets opportunities to learn, grow and succeed.
